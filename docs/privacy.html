HouseSigma Compare — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

HouseSigma Compare is an unofficial browser extension that helps you save and compare HouseSigma property listings. This policy describes what the extension does with information on your device.

Summary

  • Data is stored locally in your browser (Chrome storage on your computer).
  • There is no developer-operated server that receives your property list.
  • We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Information the extension accesses

When you use the extension on HouseSigma listing pages you open yourself, it may read:

  • Listing details shown on the page (address, price, schools, demographics, photos, etc.)
  • The page URL of the listing

The extension only runs on housesigma.com and www.housesigma.com when you visit those pages. It does not run on other websites.

Information you enter

  • Notes and scores you add in the comparison table
  • Viewed checkmarks per property
  • Commute destination address (for drive-time estimates and transit links)
  • Column and chart preferences (visible fields, widths, order)

Where data is stored

Saved properties, settings, and backups are kept in chrome.storage.local on your device. Export/import backup files are created only when you choose those actions.

Third-party services

The extension may contact these services only when you use related features:

  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org) — geocodes your commute destination address to coordinates.
  • OSRM demo router (router.project-osrm.org) — estimates driving time between a listing and your commute destination.
  • HouseSigma — pages you load in your browser; the extension reads data from pages you are already viewing while logged in as you normally would on HouseSigma.

Requests to Nominatim and OSRM include coordinates or addresses needed for routing; they do not include your full saved property list.

Permissions

  • storage — save your comparison list locally
  • activeTab / tabs — communicate with the HouseSigma tab when you use the popup
  • Host access — HouseSigma (read listings), Nominatim and OSRM (commute features only)

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.

Contact

For privacy questions about this extension, open an issue in the project repository linked from the Chrome Web Store listing, or contact the publisher email shown on the store page.

Disclaimer

HouseSigma Compare is not affiliated with or endorsed by HouseSigma. Use of HouseSigma data remains subject to HouseSigma’s own terms and policies.
