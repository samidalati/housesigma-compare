# HouseSigma Compare — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 22, 2026

HouseSigma Compare is an unofficial browser extension that helps you save and compare HouseSigma property listings. This policy describes what the extension does with information on your device.

## Summary

- Data is stored **locally in your browser** (Chrome storage on your computer).
- There is **no developer-operated server** that receives your property list.
- We do **not** sell or share your data for advertising.

## Information the extension accesses

When you use the extension on HouseSigma listing pages you open yourself, it may read:

- Listing details shown on the page (address, price, schools, demographics, photos, etc.)
- The page URL of the listing

The extension only runs on `housesigma.com` and `www.housesigma.com` when you visit those pages.

## Information you enter

- **Notes and scores** in the comparison table
- **Viewed** checkmarks per property
- **Commute destination address** (drive-time estimates and transit links)
- **Column and chart preferences**

## Where data is stored

Saved properties, settings, and backups are kept in **chrome.storage.local** on your device. Export/import backup files are created only when you choose those actions.

## Third-party services

| Service | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| OpenStreetMap Nominatim | Geocode commute destination |
| OSRM demo router | Driving time estimates |
| HouseSigma | Pages you load; extension reads visible listing data |

Requests to Nominatim and OSRM include only what is needed for geocoding/routing—not your full property list.

## Permissions

- **storage** — save comparison list locally
- **activeTab / tabs** — popup talks to the active HouseSigma tab
- **Host access** — HouseSigma, Nominatim, OSRM (as above)

## Disclaimer

HouseSigma Compare is **not affiliated with or endorsed by HouseSigma**. Use of HouseSigma remains subject to HouseSigma’s terms.
